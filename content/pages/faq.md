# FAQ

## How do I know if something will fit?

Please read the measurements and condition notes in each listing carefully. If you still have a sizing question, contact us before purchasing.

## Are all items one-of-one?

Yes. Most items in the shop are unique vintage or secondhand pieces, so once they are sold they are usually gone.

## Can I return vintage items?

Most items are final sale. If there is a problem with the item that was not listed or shown clearly, contact us and we will review it.

## When will my order ship?

Orders are usually processed as quickly as possible after checkout. Shipping updates will appear in your account page when available.

## Do you restock items?

Not usually. The shop is built around one-of-one pieces, so the inventory changes constantly instead of restocking the same item.
