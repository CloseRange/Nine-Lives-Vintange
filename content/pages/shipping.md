# Shipping

We pack and ship orders with care so your pieces arrive ready to wear.

## Processing

Orders are typically processed as quickly as possible after payment clears. If an order is placed on a weekend or holiday, it may take a little longer to get packed and handed off to the carrier.

## Shipping methods

Shipping options are shown at checkout when available. Rates are based on the order contents and destination.

## Tracking

When tracking information becomes available, it will be added to the order details page. If your order has shipped and you have not seen tracking yet, check back soon or reach out through the contact page.

## Notes

- Please make sure your shipping address is correct before completing checkout.
- We are not responsible for delays caused by the carrier once a package is in transit.
- If a package appears lost or delayed, contact us and we will help look into it.
