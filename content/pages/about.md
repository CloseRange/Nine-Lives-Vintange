# About

Nine Lives Vintage is built around the idea that great clothes deserve more than one life.

We source secondhand pieces with character, quality, and a little bit of personality, then present them in a way that makes the hunt feel simple and enjoyable. The goal is to keep shopping thoughtful without making it feel precious or complicated.

## What you can expect

- One-of-one vintage and secondhand finds
- Clear product photos and straightforward details
- A storefront designed to help you move quickly when something special shows up
- A focus on rewearing, restyling, and keeping clothing in circulation

## Why we do it

Fast fashion moves too quickly and leaves too much behind. We want to make it easier to choose pieces that already have a story, while still giving you a clean shopping experience and a little excitement when you find the right item.

## How we curate

Every listing is selected with wearability and condition in mind. Some items are bold, some are simple, and some are just strange enough to become your favorite thing in the closet.

We try to keep the shop balanced between everyday staples and standout pieces, so there is always something worth checking back for.
