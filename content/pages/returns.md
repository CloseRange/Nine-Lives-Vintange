# Returns

Because most of our inventory is one-of-one vintage and secondhand, returns are handled a little differently than a typical retail store.

## Final sale items

Unless otherwise noted, all vintage and secondhand items are considered final sale.

## If something is wrong

If your item arrives with an issue that was not described in the listing, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. We will review it and work with you to make it right.

## Before you order

We do our best to show condition and measurements clearly, so please read the listing details carefully before checking out.

## Questions

If you are unsure about sizing, condition, or fit, send a message before purchasing. We are happy to help whenever we can.
